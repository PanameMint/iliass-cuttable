Step 1

the playbook, decoded

Real estate in Melbourne, commission-only. Every deal is really two sales.

First I win the listing: prospect. find vendors. build trust. stay in touch, sometimes for years. "Hey Mia, Zoe's first week at kindergarten. How are you holding up?" pitch. list. "No I won't do it for less than 1.8%."

Then the sale itself starts: opens. calls. "The kitchen's a bit small." match the right buyer. "Would they take $100K below?" laugh awkwardly. negotiate. hard. "Congrats Emma, champagne." 30-35 of these, every year.

Networking: more calls. brokers. conveyancers. "Hey Jake, how about coffee on Tuesday?". other agents. referrals from past clients. "Sh**, I forgot school pick up time again."

Step 2

the track record, organised

Sales volume: $17.9M in 2024. $42.6M in 2025. $21.1M so far in 2026. $1.26M in gross commission introduced. Experience built over five plus years across Melbourne and Brisbane, through volatile markets.

10-14 days on average, from first conversation to signed listing. 3 months from listing to settlement. Quarterly check-ins for anyone not ready yet, some take years to convert, and that's fine.

Step 3

my skills & instincts become your pipeline

Real estate taught me how to come up with my own leads. I built the whole pipeline from zero to 900. I've already got the sales skills and the instincts, ready to take on the next challenge. Set me a target and put me somewhere selling a product I actually believe in, and I'll keep showing up for it, we might even have some fun along the way.

Step 4

why cuttable, why now

AI is about to break open advertising's real bottleneck, and Cuttable's right in the middle of it. What you're building gives a growing brand the access to the kind of creative that used to be reserved for the big players. You're backed, but still early, which is exactly the stage I want to be part of, to help you build this out in the growth phase. I believe the opportunity is massive: your promise to serve the 98% who never had access to this kind of advertising before, the pie is huge, and there's a massive market for us to conquer.

I've felt that gap before when building my own brand as a real estate agent (a business within a business) and with my side quest, Sold OffMarket.

I don't buy that good ads are dead. We're just flooded with generic ones that all sound the same. Every time I hear "at brand X, we believe in X" I almost pass out. Same thing with AI-written ads right now, scroll YouTube or Meta and you can hear it, "it's not this, it's that," or "same structure, different noun". Most people using AI now are losing their own voice. In today's market, I think the way to break through is to put more creatives out there, in a real voice, and let the winners ride.

And no, I don't have a tech sales background (yet). What I do have is five years of asking people to trust me with their single biggest asset in life. Cuttable is built around a core idea: an ad only matters if a brand is actually willing to publish it. But before you get there, you need your clients to believe in you first, and trust is what I've really been selling this whole time. You didn't build this by playing it safe and neither did I. I'm ready to go All In.

Your move.
