Step 1

The playbook, decoded

Real estate in Melbourne, commission-only. Every deal is really two sales.

First I win the listing: prospect. Find vendors. Build trust. Stay in touch, sometimes for years. "Hey Christine, remember me?" Pitch. List. "No I won't do it for less than 1.8%."

Then the sale itself starts: opens. Calls. "The kitchen's a bit small." Find the right buyer. "Would they take $100K below?" Laugh awkwardly. Negotiate. Deal done. "Congrats, Emma." Champagne.

Networking: More Calls. Referrals from past clients. Conveyancers. "How about coffee on Tuesday, Jake?" Brokers. Other agents. "Sh*t, I forgot school pick up time again."

Step 4

Why Cuttable, why now

AI is about to break open advertising's real bottleneck, and Cuttable's right in the middle of it. What you're building gives a growing brand the access to the kind of creative that used to be reserved for the big players.
